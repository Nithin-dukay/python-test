# Enhanced Employment Data Analysis

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive analysis examines New Zealand employment data from 2011 to 2025, covering filled jobs and total earnings across multiple dimensions:

- **Industries:** Various sectors from Agriculture to Services
- **Demographics:** Gender and age group breakdowns
- **Geography:** Regional and territorial authority data
- **Time Series:** Quarterly data with actual, seasonally adjusted, and trend values

## Key Insights

### 1. Employment Growth
- The data shows overall employment growth across most sectors from 2011 to 2025
- Health Care and Social Assistance shows exceptional growth (+169.5%)
- Service sectors generally outpacing traditional industries

### 2. Seasonal Patterns
- Clear quarterly variations in employment, particularly in:
  - Agriculture (peak in Q1/Q4, lower in Q2/Q3)
  - Education (strong seasonal patterns aligned with academic calendar)
  - Retail and hospitality sectors

### 3. Gender Distribution
- Both male and female employment showing growth
- Male employment: +31.6% growth over the period
- Relatively balanced growth across genders

### 4. Regional Variations
- Auckland dominates in absolute employment numbers
- Regional centers showing steady growth
- Smaller territorial authorities with more volatile patterns

### 5. Data Quality
- High data completeness (91.5% of records have values)
- 8.5% suppressed data (likely for privacy/confidentiality)
- Consistent quarterly reporting across all categories
