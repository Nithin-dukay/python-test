### 1. Aging Workforce Trend
The 65+ age group employment **more than doubled** (+108.5%), indicating:
- Delayed retirement
- Policy changes (retirement age)
- Economic necessity
- Healthier aging population

### 2. Service Sector Boom
Health Care earnings grew +169.5%, reflecting:
- Aging population demands
- Sector expansion
- Wage improvements
- Increased healthcare spending

### 3. Regional Development Success
Tasman region nearly doubled employment (+95.9%), showing:
- Successful regional development
- Economic diversification
- Migration to smaller regions
- Quality of life factors

### 4. Gender Equality Achievement
Nearly identical growth rates demonstrate:
- Effective equal opportunity policies
- Balanced workforce participation
- Sustained gender parity

### 5. Wage Growth Outpacing Employment
Earnings grew +117.7% vs employment +30.8%:
- Real wage increases
- Productivity improvements
- Economic prosperity
- Skill premium growth
